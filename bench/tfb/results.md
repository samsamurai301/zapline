## Setup

- Machine: Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz, 4 CPUs, 15 GB RAM, Linux 6.18.44-fc-v153
- Pools: `per-worker` is one pool of 16 connections per worker thread with no ping on checkout (the default); `per-worker-ping` adds sqlx's ping on every checkout; `shared` is `App::database` + `Db`: one pool of 32 connections opened on the startup runtime, with sqlx's defaults (ping included)
- App: 2 workers on CPUs 0,1; rustc 1.97.0 (2d8144b78 2026-07-07)
- Postgres 16.15 (Ubuntu 16.15-0ubuntu0.24.04.1) on CPUs 2 with TFB's postgresql.conf
- wrk debian/4.1.0-4build2: 1 thread(s), 512 connections, on CPUs 3; 5s warm-up then one 15s run per test
- Load average (1/5/15 min) 5.64 9.81 6.94 at the start, 30.36 21.53 12.63 at the end of the load runs
- App, database and load generator share one machine, so compare columns with each other, not with published TFB rounds.
